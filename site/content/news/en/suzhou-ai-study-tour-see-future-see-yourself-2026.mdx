**October 22–23, 2026 · Suzhou**

AI is moving into factories and into everyday work. For entrepreneurs, understanding both changes takes more than watching demonstrations on a screen. It helps to visit the companies building them and speak with the people involved.

Under the theme “See the Future, See Yourself,” the MindsLeap Founders AI Club brought entrepreneurs to Suzhou for a two-day study tour. The October 22 program included visits to Ruyi Dynamics and Elite Robots, an introduction to the local embodied AI ecosystem, and sessions on AI Agents and short-video marketing workflows. The following day offered a quieter setting for reflection in the Huashan scenic area.

## Ruyi Dynamics: A Founder’s View of Embodied AI

At Ruyi Dynamics (如一动力), founder and CEO Zhang Hanwen introduced the development of embodied AI and the venture his team is building.

Speaking directly with a founder brought an emerging field closer to practical business questions: how does a technology become a product, and how does that product find its place in a real application? The visit gave participants an opportunity to understand the industrial setting around embodied AI entrepreneurship, rather than encountering it only as a new concept.

![Zhang Hanwen, founder and CEO of Ruyi Dynamics, introduces the venture to MindsLeap Founders AI Club members](/images/news/suzhou-ai-study-tour-2026-ruyi-founder.jpg)

The opportunity in embodied AI is not only about a robot’s appearance or demonstration. It also depends on the problems it can address in a specific setting. Looking for that connection between future possibilities and work already underway became a recurring theme throughout the day.

![MindsLeap Founders AI Club members after their visit to Ruyi Dynamics](/images/news/suzhou-ai-study-tour-2026-ruyi-group.jpg)

## Elite Robots: From the Showroom to Production and Testing

At Elite Robots (艾利特机器人), Jiang Xushang, who heads the Suzhou company, guided the group through the showroom and the production and testing workshops. He introduced practical robot applications in pharmaceuticals, semiconductors and automotive industries.

Moving from the showroom into the workshops shifted the focus from what robots can do to how they operate within a business. Production processes, deployment settings and application requirements provided a more concrete reference for understanding the technology.

![MindsLeap Founders AI Club members learn about robotics and industry applications in the Elite Robots showroom](/images/news/suzhou-ai-study-tour-2026-elite-visit.jpg)

For entrepreneurs from different sectors, the visit offered a useful perspective: evaluate AI and robotics against real tasks and delivery requirements, not terminology alone. Emerging embodied AI approaches and established industrial robot applications also need to be understood within their different levels of maturity and use cases.

## Understanding the Industrial Ecosystem Around the Companies

Li Chenglin, responsible for investment attraction at the China-Singapore embodied intelligence incubation park, introduced the park’s planning, current ecosystem and supporting industrial policies.

His presentation placed the company visits within a wider setting, helping participants understand how innovation projects connect with park services, industry resources and application opportunities.

![Li Chenglin introduces the park’s planning, embodied intelligence ecosystem and supporting policies](/images/news/suzhou-ai-study-tour-2026-park-sharing.jpg)

Bringing a new project from technical exploration into business often requires support beyond the founding team. The discussion offered a more concrete understanding of the park’s role in connecting companies and resources, and an introduction to Suzhou’s industrial environment.

## Bringing AI Agent Opportunities Back to Everyday Business

Lincoln Wang, Founder and CEO of MindsLeap and Partner and CEO of Founders Space China, shared trends and opportunities in the emerging AI Agent business era.

As AI develops from a tool that answers questions into personal assistants and digital employees that can participate in ongoing tasks, businesses have new reasons to reconsider how they assign work, organize processes and direct human attention.

![Lincoln discusses AI Agent trends and business opportunities during the Suzhou study tour](/images/news/suzhou-ai-study-tour-2026-lincoln.jpg)

This connected with the company visits: whether robots are working in physical environments or Agents are entering office and marketing processes, the business question is how the technology fits a real situation. A useful next step is to identify a specific task to try, together with a clear way to assess its results.

## Making Short-Video Marketing a Repeatable AI Workflow

Yusi’s session brought the discussion into content production, explaining how to build an AI-assisted short-video marketing workflow.

The session covered four practical ways to reduce unsuccessful generation: divide a video into short shots and remake only the parts that need improvement; review low-resolution samples before committing to final output; create storyboard images to establish subjects, settings and composition; and provide specific visual, motion and audio references.

![Yusi presents four methods for reducing unsuccessful generation in AI-assisted short-video workflows](/images/news/suzhou-ai-study-tour-2026-yusi.jpg)

These approaches balance quality, control and cost. Rather than expecting a perfect result from a single generation, teams can work through smaller pieces, previews and targeted revisions. For companies producing marketing content regularly, that is closer to a repeatable working process than a one-off experiment.

## A Quieter Day at Huashan

On October 23, the group visited Suzhou’s Huashan scenic area and stayed at Huashan Yinju (花山隐居), with hiking, tea, meditation and sutra copying. After a day looking outward at technology and industry, the second day left space to reflect on personal priorities. Together, the two days gave the theme “See the Future, See Yourself” a practical shape.
