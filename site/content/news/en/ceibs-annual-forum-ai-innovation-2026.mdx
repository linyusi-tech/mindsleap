**On November 7, 2026, Steve Hoffman, founder of Founders Space and author of *Make Elephants Fly*, delivered a keynote at the CEIBS Alumni Reunion and 2026 Annual Forum. He also joined CEIBS Professor of Economics Lan Xiaohuan, author of *How China Works*, in a dialogue on AI and business innovation.**

Held at CEIBS' Shanghai campus under the theme *Rising Winds, Diverging Futures*, the forum connected global change with technological innovation and business. Lincoln Wang (王林), Founder and CEO of MindsLeap and Partner at Founders Space, attended by invitation.

For entrepreneurs, AI is more than a technology-sector story. It affects how work gets done, how services are priced and how companies understand their strengths. These questions underpin MindsLeap's interest in connecting international innovation perspectives with business practice in China.

## Steve Hoffman: Connecting AI Progress With Business Value

Hoffman's keynote examined China from a Silicon Valley venture-capital perspective and considered investment opportunities amid transformation.

According to [CEIBS' coverage](https://www.ceibs.edu/media/news/events-visits/30305), he discussed collaborating agents, personal AI assistants and AI-native businesses. As AI takes on tasks, he suggested that companies can increasingly build services around completed work and measurable outcomes.

He also addressed employment, human agency and cooperation on AI safety, placing the human implications alongside commercial opportunities.

For business leaders, this is a useful distinction: understanding a tool's capabilities is not the same as establishing what customers need, how a service will be delivered and how its results will be assessed. Those questions belong in the business itself.

## A Dialogue With Lan Xiaohuan: More Than One Perspective on AI

In *The Next Round of the China–US AI Race*, Hoffman joined Lan Xiaohuan in a conversation moderated by Gu Bin, a former CCTV host and CEIBS alumnus.

![Gu Bin moderates a dialogue between Lan Xiaohuan and Steve Hoffman at the CEIBS Annual Forum](/images/news/ceibs-annual-forum-ai-innovation-2026-panel.jpg)

Lan cautioned against prematurely declaring a winner in AI. He called for considering computing, chips and electricity together, and highlighted the importance of people willing to express different views.

Hoffman discussed how costs, data security and trust influence adoption, encouraging businesses to use AI to reconsider established assumptions and business models.

![Steve Hoffman discusses AI during the dialogue at the CEIBS Annual Forum](/images/news/ceibs-annual-forum-ai-innovation-2026-hoffman-dialogue.jpg)

A valuable conversation does not have to produce a single answer. Bringing different perspectives together can help entrepreneurs see the conditions surrounding a technology and exercise their own judgment.

## From a Forum Conversation to a Practical Next Step

For MindsLeap, participating in such discussions helps keep a global perspective connected to business realities.

Moving from an idea to a useful change still requires practical work: choose a task worth testing, identify the information and permissions it needs, define acceptance criteria, and refine the approach through use. External perspectives provide reference points; each company's customers, operations and people determine its next step.

Through focused discussions, company visits and peer learning, MindsLeap will continue helping entrepreneurs understand AI and turn that understanding into actions suited to their businesses.
