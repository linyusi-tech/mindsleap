**August 19, 2026 | Beijing · Zhipu AI**

On August 19, 2026, the MindsLeap Founders AI Club organized a visit to Zhipu AI for an in-person exchange on large model technology, commercialization, enterprise use cases, and AI transformation.

This was not simply a product briefing. The visit began with a tour of Zhipu AI's exhibition hall, where participating entrepreneurs learned about the company's work across foundation models, agent applications, enterprise services, and industry scenarios. For many founders and business leaders, large models are usually understood through product interfaces, launch events, or industry reports. Walking into a company that builds foundation models and works with enterprise applications makes it easier to see how technical capability, product systems, and business scenarios connect.

## From Model Evolution to Enterprise Use Cases

In the following session, Zhipu AI experts shared how large model capabilities have evolved, from conversation and coding to long-context understanding and more complex task execution. The discussion did not stop at how powerful a model can be. It kept returning to practical enterprise questions: How does AI enter real business workflows? Which scenarios are suitable for large models? How should companies balance capability, cost, data security, and implementation results?

![Zhipu AI experts shared perspectives on AI value, model boundaries, and practical enterprise application paths](/images/news/zhipu-ai-founders-ai-club-visit-2026-sharing-v2.jpg)

A second discussion focused on commercialization and application scenarios. Zhipu AI experts introduced several common enterprise paths, including API usage, team subscriptions, private deployment, knowledge bases, agent collaboration, and industry applications. Entrepreneurs in the room raised concrete questions around healthcare, finance, semiconductor-related scenarios, enterprise knowledge management, and data security.

For public publication, we have intentionally avoided specific figures, pricing, customer details, and internal deployment information. What matters more is the shared question behind the conversation: how can companies move AI from something that looks impressive to something that creates real business outcomes?

## AI Adoption Is a Business Judgment, Not Just Tool Procurement

For MindsLeap, the most valuable part of the visit was not only learning about the latest progress of a leading large model company. It was helping entrepreneurs bring AI back from tool experimentation to business judgment.

The central question for companies today is no longer whether to use AI. It is where AI can create real business results. If a company only lets employees use AI tools in scattered ways, it is hard to create organization-level change. The more important work is to rethink roles, workflows, data, and collaboration so AI becomes part of the operating system of the company, with digital employees and agents participating in real business processes.

![During the exchange, entrepreneurs discussed AI implementation scenarios, organizational collaboration, and application boundaries with Zhipu AI experts](/images/news/zhipu-ai-founders-ai-club-visit-2026-qa.jpg)

This is why the MindsLeap Founders AI Club continues to organize visits and closed-door discussions with companies working at the front line of AI. Entrepreneurs need access to leading AI teams, but they also need to translate technical change into their own business language: which capabilities are already mature, which are still demonstrations, which can be tested in small pilots, and which deserve to become part of company-level strategy.

## Building a More Grounded AI Judgment

Over the past year, many companies have started experimenting with AI. The real challenge is no longer opening a tool. It is building judgment. Business leaders need to evaluate model capability, cost structure, data security, organizational readiness, and business return. They also need to find a concrete, frequent, and verifiable scenario where AI can begin to become part of a workflow.

The MindsLeap Founders AI Club aims to bring entrepreneurs closer to the real AI field. By visiting frontline AI companies, industry sites, and innovation organizations, members can see not only the technology itself, but also the business, organizational, and collaboration questions that emerge when AI enters companies.

MindsLeap will continue leading member companies into more AI-native companies and industrial scenarios through visits, discussions, workshops, and hands-on programs, helping entrepreneurs build more grounded AI judgment and turn that judgment into executable AI transformation actions inside their companies.
