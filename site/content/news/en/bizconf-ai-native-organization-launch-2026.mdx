**AI can help an individual work faster. But what does it take to help an entire organization work better? On November 12, 2026, Steve Hoffman and Lincoln Wang attended the task-management-native Agent launch hosted by publicly listed BizConf (会畅科技), with discussions focused on AI-native enterprises and organizational collaboration.**

Steve Hoffman, Chairman and CEO of Founders Space, shared perspectives on building AI-native enterprises. Lincoln Wang, Founder and CEO of MindsLeap and Partner at Founders Space, moderated a roundtable on moving from individual productivity gains to organizational efficiency.

At the event, BizConf introduced its enterprise task-management Agent, Baize (白泽), and an Agent-enabled device for executive offices, Chaoguang (超光), demonstrating connections between meetings, tasks, and execution.

## Hoffman Brings AI-Native Enterprise Building into the Discussion

Hoffman's session addressed the construction of AI-native enterprises, bringing a Silicon Valley entrepreneurship and innovation perspective to the event.

The subject raises questions beyond whether employees can use AI tools: which tasks can be delegated to Agents, how people and Agents divide responsibilities, and how new forms of collaboration enter everyday operations.

These questions also inform MindsLeap's ongoing enterprise AI transformation discussions. Technical progress creates possibilities; integrating those capabilities into an organization requires attention to specific tasks and working relationships.

## Lincoln Moderates a Roundtable on Organizational Efficiency

The roundtable brought together Yang Hua (杨桦), Senior Vice President of CloudWalk; Yang Ye (杨烨), CHRO of Aumovio China; Ni Yiwen (倪奕雯), a management consulting partner; and Wu Mingyuan (吴鸣远), chief computer-sector analyst at Huachuang Securities.

![MindsLeap Founder and CEO Lincoln Wang moderates the AI-native organizational transformation roundtable at the BizConf launch](/images/news/bizconf-ai-native-organization-launch-2026-lincoln.jpg)

According to the organizer's event release, participants recognized visible gains in individual productivity while identifying unresolved organizational challenges: aligning goals, maintaining task records, establishing evaluation criteria, and addressing coordination bottlenecks.

A document completed faster does not guarantee a smooth handoff. A meeting action item does not guarantee execution. The discussion therefore moved beyond what AI can do to how work is taken up, advanced, and reviewed.

Connecting goals, execution, and feedback through tasks was a central theme of the roundtable.

![Lincoln Wang and fellow panelists discuss the shift from individual productivity to organizational efficiency](/images/news/bizconf-ai-native-organization-launch-2026-panel.jpg)

## Connecting Meeting Actions with Execution and Feedback

BizConf described Baize as an enterprise task-intelligence hub addressing meeting information, task decomposition and routing, and strategic alignment. Chaoguang targets executive-office interactions, with demonstrations covering task-progress queries, scheduling suggestions, meeting bookings, and task reminders.

For managers, the practical questions go beyond specifications: can meeting decisions enter ongoing workflows? Can execution problems be identified promptly? Do managers have enough information to decide what happens next?

![BizConf demonstrates its Chaoguang Agent-enabled executive-office device at the launch event](/images/news/bizconf-ai-native-organization-launch-2026-launch.jpg)
